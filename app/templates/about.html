Core System Features

Built from the ground up to deliver a seamless end-to-end shopping experience.

AI Shopping Assistant

Understands natural language shopping queries (e.g. "Show me laptops under ₹60,000 for coding") and ranks relevant products dynamically.

Curated Dataset & Catalog

Indexed real product records with rich specifications, categories, INR pricing, ratings, and instant search & filtering.

Wishlist & Cart Pipeline

Database-backed wishlist toggle state (`♥` / `♡`) and unified shopping cart persistence connected directly to MySQL.

Orders & Checkout

Complete checkout pipeline generating unique order numbers (`ORD-XXXX`), shipping tracking, and order history logs.

Compare Specs & Planner

Side-by-side product specification comparison table and shopping planner budget cap calculator.

Admin Management

Role-based access control (`@admin_required`) allowing administrators to create, edit, delete products and update order statuses.
